# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@progrid.co** with a description, the steps to reproduce and the impact you
expect. Please do not open a public issue, and do not access data that is not yours, degrade the
service or run automated scanners against production at high volume.

We acknowledge reports within 2 business days, keep you informed while we fix the issue and
credit you when it is published, if you wish. We will not take legal action against research done
in good faith within these rules.

## Supported versions

Only the hosted service and the latest release of the CLI, SDKs and Terraform provider receive
security fixes.
