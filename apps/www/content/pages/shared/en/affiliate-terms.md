## 1. The program

The Progrid affiliate program lets approved partners earn a commission on payments from customers they refer. Joining is free. Progrid may accept or decline any application and may change or end the program with notice on this page.

## 2. Who is a referred customer

A customer is referred by you only when they sign up for Progrid with your partner code, or add your code in their billing settings before their first paid invoice. Your referral link fills in your code on the signup form for a limited time after a click; a customer who removes the code is not referred. A customer account can be linked to one partner only, and the link does not change later.

## 3. Commission

- Commission is a percentage of the amount a referred customer actually pays, excluding VAT and other taxes, discounts, and free or promotional credit. Rates per product are published on the [affiliate program page](/affiliates) and apply from the time an invoice is paid.
- Commission is earned on invoices for the first 12 months after the customer used your code, unless the program page says otherwise.
- Commission is pending for a hold period (60 days unless the program page says otherwise) after the invoice is paid, then becomes payable.
- Refunds, credit notes, cancelled invoices and chargebacks reverse the related commission, including commission already paid, which is deducted from later payouts.

## 4. Payouts

- Commission is earned in the currency of the customer invoice: US dollars or Saudi riyals. All commission is paid by Progrid Arabia, the company that runs the program.
- You can request a payout when your payable balance in a currency reaches the minimum (200 SAR or 50 USD). Payouts are made by bank transfer to the account in your payout details.
- You are responsible for your own taxes. Progrid may ask for tax forms or identity documents before paying.

## 5. Rules

You may not:

- refer yourself, your own accounts or businesses you control;
- bid on Progrid brand terms in search advertising, or use them in domain names or social handles;
- send spam or unsolicited messages, or publish your code on coupon sites without your own content;
- make misleading claims about Progrid, its prices, its locations or its features;
- pretend to speak for Progrid or use its logos beyond the assets provided in the affiliate portal.

## 6. Disclosure

Tell your audience clearly, close to your link or code, that you earn a commission from Progrid. This is required by consumer protection rules in most countries.

## 7. Suspension and termination

Progrid may suspend an affiliate, hold payouts or cancel commission when it detects fraud, abuse or a breach of these rules. Either side can leave the program at any time; payable commission earned under these rules is still paid.
