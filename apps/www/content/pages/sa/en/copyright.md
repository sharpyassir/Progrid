Progrid respects intellectual property rights and expects its customers to do the same. This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms) and the [Acceptable use policy](/legal/acceptable-use). It applies to content hosted on Progrid that infringes copyright protected under the Copyright Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, or trademarks or other intellectual property rights.

Progrid provides infrastructure. Our customers control the content on their servers and buckets, and in most cases the customer is best placed to remove it. This policy does not replace the procedures of the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) or the competent courts, which remain available to rights holders.

## 1. How to send a notice

Send your notice to **support@progrid.sa** with "Copyright notice" in the subject line. A complete notice includes:

1. your name, address, email address and phone number, and, if you act for the rights holder, your authority to do so;
2. a description of the work or right that you say is infringed;
3. evidence that you own the right or are authorized by its owner, such as registration with SAIP where available;
4. the exact location of the content on Progrid: the URL, the IP address, or the domain, and the date and time you saw it;
5. an explanation of why the use is not authorized by the rights holder or by law;
6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and given in good faith;
7. your signature, which may be an electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may delay our response. We may ask for more information.

## 2. What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we:

1. forward it to the customer concerned and ask them to remove the content or respond, normally within 2 business days;
2. remove or disable access to the content ourselves if the customer does not act, if the infringement is clear, or if a competent authority orders it;
3. tell the person who sent the notice what action was taken.

We may share the notice, including your name and contact details, with the customer so they can respond, unless you ask us not to and the law allows it.

## 3. Counter notice

If you are a customer and you believe that content was removed by mistake or that you have the right to use it, you may send a counter notice to support@progrid.sa with "Copyright counter notice" in the subject line. It must include your account details, the content removed and its former location, the reasons you have the right to use it, supporting evidence, a statement that the information is accurate and given in good faith, and your signature.

We send the counter notice to the person who sent the original notice. We may restore the content unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have started proceedings before SAIP or a competent court. Our decision does not decide the dispute between the parties.

## 4. Repeat infringers

We terminate the accounts of customers who repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights, in line with section 12 of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). Serious or deliberate infringement, such as hosting pirated software or media for distribution, may lead to immediate suspension and termination without prior warning.

## 5. False notices

Do not send notices that you know to be false or misleading, or that target lawful use. Abusive notices may be ignored, and the person sending them may be liable for the damage they cause.
