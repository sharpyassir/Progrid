This Data processing addendum ("Addendum") is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms) between the customer ("you") and Progrid Arabia ("Progrid", "we"). It applies when you use the Services to store or process personal data of other people, such as your own customers, users or staff. You accept it when you accept the Terms of service. It does not cover the personal data that Progrid processes as a controller, which is described in the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. Definitions

Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject" and "personal data breach" have the meaning given in the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations (together, the "PDPL"). "Customer personal data" means personal data contained in the content you store or process on the Services. "Subprocessor" means a third party that Progrid engages to process customer personal data.

## 2. Roles

2.1 For customer personal data, you are the controller (or a processor acting for another controller) and Progrid is your processor.

2.2 You are responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing, for giving the notices and obtaining the consents that the law requires, for the accuracy of the data, and for deciding whether the Services, including hosting outside the Kingdom, are suitable for the data you process.

2.3 You must not store on the Services data that the law requires to be kept inside the Kingdom, or data classified by a competent authority as not allowed to be hosted outside it.

## 3. Your instructions

3.1 We process customer personal data only on your documented instructions. The Agreement, your configuration of the Services, and your actions in the console and through the API are your instructions. Additional instructions must be consistent with the Agreement.

3.2 We may process customer personal data without your instructions where the law requires it. In that case we tell you first, unless the law prevents it.

3.3 We tell you if we believe an instruction breaks the PDPL or other law, and we may refuse to follow it.

## 4. Confidentiality

Progrid ensures that its staff and contractors who may access customer personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations, receive appropriate training, and access the data only as needed to provide the Services.

## 5. Security

5.1 We implement the technical and organizational measures in Annex 2 to protect customer personal data against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage.

5.2 You are responsible for the security of what you control: your operating systems, software, configuration, firewall rules, credentials and tokens, encryption of data at rest where you need it, and backups. You decide which security features of the Services to use.

5.3 We may update the measures in Annex 2, provided that the overall level of protection does not decrease.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 You authorize Progrid to engage the subprocessors listed on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page. We bind each subprocessor by written contract to data protection obligations no less protective than this Addendum, and we remain responsible for their performance.

6.2 We notify you of a new subprocessor at least 30 days before it starts processing customer personal data, by email and on the Subprocessors page.

6.3 You may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by writing to support@progrid.sa within that 30 day period. We will work with you in good faith to find a solution. If we cannot, you may terminate the affected Services before the change takes effect, and we refund any prepaid amount for the period after termination.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, we help you respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under the PDPL. Most requests can be handled directly by you, since you control the data on your resources. If we receive a request directly, we forward it to you and do not answer it ourselves, unless the law requires us to.

7.2 We provide reasonable information to help you carry out impact assessments and consult the competent authority where the PDPL requires. Assistance that goes beyond the standard features of the Services may be charged at our usual rates.

## 8. Personal data breaches

8.1 We notify you of a personal data breach affecting customer personal data without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where feasible, so that you can meet your own obligations to notify SDAIA and data subjects.

8.2 The notice describes, as far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of records and data subjects concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. We provide further information as it becomes available.

8.3 Our notice is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 9. Transfers outside the Kingdom

9.1 You acknowledge that the Services are hosted outside the Kingdom, as described in Annex 1, and that subprocessors may process data in the countries listed on the Subprocessors page.

9.2 We transfer customer personal data outside the Kingdom only as permitted by the PDPL and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom, using the grounds and safeguards described in the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy), including contractual safeguards with each subprocessor.

9.3 You are responsible for meeting the transfer obligations that apply to you as controller, including any transfer risk assessment and any notice to or consent from data subjects.

## 10. Return and deletion

10.1 You can export or delete customer personal data at any time using the Services.

10.2 When you delete a resource, or at the latest 30 days after your account is closed, we delete customer personal data on that resource, except backups, which expire on their normal schedule, and data that the law requires us to keep.

## 11. Audits and information

11.1 We make available to you the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum, including this document, our security measures and, where available, reports or certifications of our subprocessors.

11.2 If that information is not enough to meet a requirement of the PDPL or of a competent authority, you may request an audit by written notice at least 30 days in advance. Audits are limited to once a year unless required by a competent authority or following a personal data breach, are carried out during business hours by you or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, must not disrupt the Services or compromise the data of other customers, and are at your cost.

## 12. Requests from authorities

If we receive a request from an authority for customer personal data, we handle it as described in our [Law enforcement guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement), redirect the authority to you where possible, and notify you unless the law prevents it.

## 13. Liability and precedence

13.1 Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations and exclusions in the [Terms of service](/legal/terms).

13.2 In case of conflict between this Addendum and the Terms of service on the processing of customer personal data, this Addendum prevails.

13.3 This Addendum stays in force for as long as Progrid processes customer personal data.

## Annex 2. Technical and organizational measures

**Encryption.** Data is encrypted in transit between users and the platform using current TLS. Traffic between our control plane and our hypervisor servers runs through an encrypted WireGuard tunnel; some traffic between our components on the same private network is not yet encrypted, and we are adding encryption to it. Passwords are stored as hashes. Credentials and other secrets that the Services keep for you are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, under keys kept separately from the database. Backups of the control plane are encrypted with age before they leave our servers. Full disk encryption of our servers' storage is planned and not yet in place; you can encrypt your own volumes and databases.

**Access control.** Access to production systems is limited to named personnel who need it, and is reviewed every quarter. Administrative access requires two step authentication. Engineers reach servers covered by a managed service only through time limited, approved access, with short lived certificates issued per session through a gateway, not with shared or long lived keys. Access to our own servers uses personal keys over restricted network paths, and password login to servers is disabled.

**Recorded sessions.** Engineer sessions on servers covered by a managed service are recorded, kept for 12 months and can be reviewed.

**Audit logs.** Every change made through the console and the API, and every read by our staff in our administrative tools, is written to audit logs that cannot be changed without detection and are kept for 1 year. Customers can see the audit log of their own account.

**Network security.** Host firewalls deny inbound traffic by default. Management interfaces of our servers are reachable only over a private, encrypted network. Each customer server runs in its own virtual machine, and firewall rules on the hypervisor, outside your control, restrict traffic between different customers' networks.

**Backups.** Platform data is backed up every night and the backups are encrypted. Off site copies and monthly restore tests are being introduced. Snapshots and backups of your resources are available as features of the Services; snapshots are stored on the same infrastructure as the resource.

**Infrastructure.** Our hosting providers, DigitalOcean in Frankfurt and Hetzner Online in Germany, operate data centers with physical access control, power and cooling redundancy and environmental protection. Storage devices that leave our control are erased before they are returned to the provider.

**Vulnerability management.** Operating system security updates are installed automatically on our servers. Our code, dependencies and container images are scanned automatically before release, and vulnerabilities are fixed within defined periods according to their severity. Security reports are acknowledged within two business days.

**Incident response.** We have a documented plan to detect, contain, investigate and notify personal data breaches, as described in section 8.

**Staff.** We require staff with access to personal data to be bound by confidentiality, to be screened before they receive access as the law permits, and to be trained on security and data protection when they join and every year.

**Subprocessors.** We assess the security of our subprocessors, bind them by written data protection obligations, and review them every year.
