This policy explains how Progrid Arabia processes personal data about our customers, their team members, website visitors, affiliates and people who contact us. It is written to meet the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (PDPL), its Implementing Regulations and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom.

This policy does not cover the content that customers store or process on their resources. For that content the customer is the controller and Progrid is a processor, under the [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa).

## 2. What we collect

**Account data.** Name, email address, password (stored as a hash), phone number if you add one, company name, billing country, tax number and address for invoicing, team membership and roles.

**Sign in with Google or Microsoft.** If you choose to sign in with one of them, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider. We do not receive your password.

**Verification and fraud prevention data.** The identity or business documents you provide for verification, and the results of fraud and risk checks on accounts and payments: IP address and approximate location, device and browser signals, payment outcome and risk signals from our payment provider, links between accounts, and records of past breaches of our terms.

**Legal acceptance record.** Each time you accept the Terms of service, the Acceptable use policy and this Privacy policy, we record the document and its version, the date and time, your IP address and your user agent.

**Billing data.** Invoices, payments, credit history and affiliate payouts. Payments by mada, card and Apple Pay are processed by our payment provider; we receive a reference, the last four digits and the card brand, never the full number. For affiliates, the bank details you give for payouts.

**Usage and technical data.** API requests, console actions, IP addresses, browser and device information, and an audit log of who did what on your account, including API tokens and the AI tools you connect. Server metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network) are collected to run the platform and show you graphs.

**Integrations.** If you connect GitHub for deployments, we receive your GitHub account identifier and an access token limited to the repositories you choose.

**Alerts.** If you use managed cloud on call alerts, the phone numbers you add to receive SMS or WhatsApp messages.

**Progrid Connect.** The instructions, inputs and outputs of the AI agents you run, as needed to run them and show you their history.

**Support data.** Tickets, messages and the details you give us to solve a problem.

**Content you store.** Whatever you put on your servers, volumes, databases and buckets is yours. We do not read it, index it or use it for any purpose other than storing and serving it, except as needed to respond to your support request, to perform a managed service you ordered, or as required by law.

## 3. Why we process it and on what basis

Under the PDPL we process personal data only for a clear purpose and on a lawful basis:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and running your account, providing the Services, metering and invoicing | Performance of the agreement you are party to |
| Recording your acceptance of our terms and policies | Performance of the agreement, our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in proving the agreement |
| Verification, fraud prevention, enforcing bans, platform and network security, incident response | Our legitimate interest in a secure platform free of fraud, and our legal obligations |
| ZATCA e-invoicing, tax and accounting records, responding to lawful requests from competent authorities | Legal obligations |
| Service notices, invoices, security alerts and answers to your requests | Performance of the agreement |
| Marketing email | Your consent, which you can withdraw from any message |
| Improving the Services with aggregated and de-identified data | Our legitimate interest |

We rely on legitimate interest only where the PDPL and its regulations allow it, never for sensitive data, and only where it does not override your rights. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effect on you, except that automated fraud checks may hold a payment or an account for human review.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed for the purposes above:

- **Service providers** that host the platform, send email and messages, process payments and run AI models, under contracts that bind them to protect the data and to use it only on our instructions. The full list, with locations, is on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.
- **The Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority**, for e-invoicing, as the law requires.
- **Competent authorities**, when a lawful request or court order requires it, or to report fraud and crime as the law allows. See our [Law enforcement guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement). We notify you unless the law prevents it.
- **Payment providers and card networks**, to prevent and resolve fraud and chargebacks.
- **Professional advisers**, such as lawyers and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality.
- **A successor**, if Progrid is acquired or merges, under the same protections.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Our platform is hosted outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

- the control plane (website, console, API and account databases) is hosted by DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany;
- customer servers and their storage run on Hetzner Online in Falkenstein, Germany.

Some service providers process data in other countries: transactional email (Resend) in Ireland, and AI models (Anthropic), SMS and WhatsApp alerts (Twilio) and sign in or Git providers (Google, Microsoft, GitHub) in the United States. Payments (Moyasar) are processed in Saudi Arabia.

## 6. Transfer outside the Kingdom

Because the platform is hosted in Germany, using the Services involves transferring personal data outside the Kingdom. We transfer personal data outside the Kingdom only as the PDPL and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom permit, and in particular:

- we transfer only the minimum personal data needed for each purpose;
- we rely on the grounds the Regulation allows, which include transfers to countries that SDAIA has recognized as providing an adequate level of protection, where such a decision exists, and otherwise appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses approved by SDAIA or other binding contractual commitments from each recipient;
- we carry out the transfer risk assessment that the Regulation requires where it applies;
- we choose providers with strong security, including encryption in transit and access controls, and we review them periodically;
- we do not transfer data where the transfer would harm national security or the vital interests of the Kingdom.

When you create an account, you are shown this policy and you acknowledge that your personal data will be transferred to and stored in the countries listed above. Where the law requires your consent to a specific transfer, we ask for it. You can ask us for more information about the safeguards for a transfer at support@progrid.sa.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account data: for as long as your account exists and for 5 years after closure, to meet accounting and tax rules, or longer where tax law requires.
- Legal acceptance records: for as long as your account exists and for 5 years after closure.
- Invoices and payment records: for the period that tax and accounting law requires, and at least 5 years.
- Audit logs: 1 year.
- Server metrics: 30 days at full resolution.
- Support tickets: 3 years.
- Verification documents: for as long as your account exists and for 5 years after closure, unless the law requires a different period.
- Records of accounts terminated for fraud or abuse: only the data needed to enforce the ban and to bring or defend legal claims, for as long as needed for those purposes [period to be confirmed].
- Content you store: deleted when you delete the resource and, at the latest, 30 days after your account is closed, except for backups, which expire on their normal schedule.

When a period ends we delete the data or make it anonymous.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Access to production systems is limited to named engineers with two step sign in and short lived certificates, and engineer sessions and administrative actions are logged. We apply host firewalls, backups and regular updates. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data in line with the PDPL and the security controls of the competent authorities that apply to us.

## 9. Personal data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, we act to contain it and assess its impact. Where the PDPL and its regulations require, we notify SDAIA within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, and we notify affected people without undue delay where the breach may harm them, with a description of the breach and the steps they can take.

## 10. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

- **be informed** of how and why your personal data is processed, which is the purpose of this policy;
- **access** your personal data that we hold;
- **obtain a copy** of it in a clear and readable format;
- **correct** data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
- **delete** data we no longer need, unless the law requires us to keep it;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time where we process data based on consent, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.

Send requests to support@progrid.sa from the email address on your account. We confirm your identity before releasing any data. We answer within 30 days, or tell you within that time if we need longer as the regulations allow. Most account data can also be viewed and corrected directly in the console.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), the competent authority under the PDPL.

## 11. Children

The Services are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we close it and delete the data, except what the law requires us to keep.

## 12. Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to run the website and the console. See the [Cookie policy](/legal/cookies).

## 13. Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes. We notify material changes by email and in the console at least 30 days before they take effect, except where a change is needed immediately for legal or security reasons. Where a new version must be accepted, you are asked to accept it before you continue to use the console or the API.
