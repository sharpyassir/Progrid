This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). It explains the sanctions and export control rules that Progrid Arabia ("Progrid") follows and what they mean for you.

## 1. Rules we follow

Progrid complies with:

- sanctions adopted by the United Nations Security Council;
- the laws and measures of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including measures on terrorism, its financing and the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, and the lists issued by the competent Saudi authorities;
- the sanctions and export control regimes of the United States, the European Union and the United Kingdom, to the extent that they apply to us, to our infrastructure and service providers, to our payment providers and card networks, or to the software and technology we use.

Our infrastructure is provided by companies in the European Union and the United States, and payments run through international card networks, so their rules affect what we can offer even where they do not bind you directly.

## 2. Prohibited countries and regions

You may not use the Services, and we do not open or maintain accounts, for persons located in, ordinarily resident in, or organized under the laws of a country or region that is subject to comprehensive sanctions. At the date of this policy this includes Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, as updated by the authorities from time to time. We may also restrict the Services for other countries where the rules in section 1 restrict cloud or technology services.

## 3. Prohibited parties

You may not use the Services if you, or a person who owns or controls you or acts on your behalf, are:

- listed on a sanctions list of the United Nations Security Council or of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia;
- listed on a sanctions or restricted party list of the United States, the European Union or the United Kingdom that applies to us or to our providers;
- owned or controlled by, or acting for, a listed person.

## 4. Prohibited end uses

You may not use the Services, directly or indirectly, for:

- the development, production or use of nuclear, chemical or biological weapons, or missiles to deliver them;
- military or intelligence end uses that are restricted under the rules in section 1;
- providing services to, or making funds or resources available to, a prohibited country, region or party;
- evading or helping others evade sanctions or export controls, including by using false information, intermediaries or tools that hide your location.

## 5. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you are not a prohibited party and that you will not export, re-export or transfer anything you obtain through the Services in breach of these rules. You are responsible for your own compliance and for your users. Tell us at once at support@progrid.sa if any of this changes.

## 6. What we do

To comply with these rules, we may screen accounts and payments against sanctions lists, check location signals such as IP addresses and billing details, and ask for more information. We may, without liability to you and without prior notice where the law requires:

- block access from prohibited countries and regions;
- refuse, suspend or terminate accounts, resources and payments;
- freeze or hold funds and report to the competent authorities where the law requires it.

Termination under this policy is treated as termination for breach under the [Terms of service](/legal/terms).
