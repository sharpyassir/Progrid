Progrid Arabia uses the service providers below to run the Services. They process personal data on our behalf, for the personal data we control as described in the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy), and for the personal data our customers store on the Services as described in the [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa).

Each provider is bound by a written contract to protect the data, to use it only to provide its service to us, and to apply security measures suitable for the data. Transfers outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia follow the safeguards described in the Privacy policy.

## Current subprocessors

| Provider | Purpose | Location of processing | When it applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| DigitalOcean | Hosting of the control plane: website, console, API and account databases | Frankfurt, Germany | Always |
| Hetzner Online GmbH | Compute and storage for customer servers and other customer resources | Falkenstein, Germany | Always, for customer resources |
| Resend | Sending transactional email, such as sign in codes, invoices and alerts | Ireland (EU region); US company | Always |
| Postmark (ActiveCampaign, LLC) | Backup provider for transactional email | United States | Only if we switch email delivery from Resend to Postmark, for example during an outage of Resend. We will update this page when it is in use |
| Moyasar | Payment processing for mada, cards and Apple Pay | Saudi Arabia | When you pay by card, mada or Apple Pay |
| Anthropic | AI model provider for Progrid Connect agents | United States | Only when you run Progrid Connect agents |
| Twilio | SMS and WhatsApp alerts for managed cloud on call | United States | Only when you use on call alerts |
| Google | Sign in with Google | United States and other locations of the provider | Only when you choose to sign in with Google |
| Microsoft | Sign in with Microsoft | United States and other locations of the provider | Only when you choose to sign in with Microsoft |
| GitHub | Git deployments from your repositories | United States | Only when you connect GitHub |
| Infisical | Secrets management for the credentials our engineers use on servers covered by a managed cloud service | United States [region to be confirmed] | Only for managed cloud services, and only if we keep those credentials in Infisical's hosted service rather than in our own secrets store |
| E-invoicing provider [name to be confirmed] | Clearance and reporting of e-invoices with ZATCA | [to be confirmed] | For invoices issued by Progrid Arabia |

For Google, Microsoft and GitHub, the data you share with that provider is also governed by its own terms and privacy policy, since you hold your own account with it.

## Changes to this list

We notify customers of a new subprocessor at least 30 days before it starts processing customer personal data, by email to account owners and by updating this page. Customers may object as described in section 6 of the [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa).

Where a change is needed urgently to keep the Services secure or available, we may make it with shorter notice and tell you as soon as possible.
