This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). It applies to everything you run on Progrid and to everyone who uses your account, including team members, your own users, scripts, and AI tools and agents acting with your tokens. The examples below are not a complete list: anything with a similar purpose or effect is also prohibited.

## 1. Illegal and harmful content and activity

You may not use the Services for anything that is illegal under the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including the Anti-Cyber Crime Law, the Personal Data Protection Law, the Electronic Transactions Law, the E-Commerce Law, the Copyright Law and the Anti Money Laundering Law, or under the laws that apply to you or your users. This includes:

- content that is contrary to public order or public morals in the Kingdom, or that is sexually explicit;
- child sexual abuse material, or any content that exploits or endangers minors in any way (we report it to the competent authorities);
- terrorism, its financing or promotion, violent extremism, and incitement to violence or hatred against any group;
- fraud and scams, including fake shops, investment and crypto scams, fake invoices and impersonation of people, companies or authorities;
- money laundering, unlicensed financial or payment services, and the sale of stolen data, credentials or payment cards;
- gambling and betting services;
- the sale of drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods or other illegal goods and services;
- infringing copyright, trademarks or other intellectual property, including pirated software, media and license keys (see the [Copyright and intellectual property policy](/legal/copyright));
- violating privacy, including collecting, publishing or selling personal data without a lawful basis, and covert surveillance;
- defamation, insults, harassment, threats, and publishing private information about others without consent;
- evading sanctions or export controls (see the [Export controls and sanctions policy](/legal/export-sanctions)).

## 2. Security attacks and network abuse

- No phishing pages, credential harvesting or fake sign in pages.
- No malware, ransomware, spyware, exploit kits, or botnets and their command and control.
- No unauthorized access to any system, network, account or data, and no scanning, probing or vulnerability testing of systems you are not authorized to test.
- No denial of service attacks, and no hosting of tools or services offered to attack others (booters and stressers).
- No credential stuffing, brute force attacks or traffic designed to bypass other services' protections.
- No IP, DNS or email spoofing, and no open mail relays, open proxies or open DNS resolvers exposed to the internet.

## 3. Spam and unsolicited messages

No bulk unsolicited email, SMS or messaging, no harvesting of addresses, and no hosting of content advertised through spam. Mailing lists must be opt in with a working unsubscribe link. Port 25 is closed by default; ask support to open it for legitimate mail servers with reverse DNS and SPF in place.

## 4. Deceptive and harmful AI use

- No deepfakes or synthetic media that impersonate a real person without consent, or that are used to deceive, defraud or defame.
- No AI generated content presented as genuine evidence, news or official communication in order to mislead.
- No use of Progrid Connect or any AI agent to do anything prohibited by this policy, including automated fraud, spam, scanning or attacks.
- No attempts to bypass the safety measures of the AI models made available through the Services.

## 5. Abuse of the platform

- No cryptocurrency mining or proof of work computation without our prior written approval.
- No attempts to bypass metering, billing, spending caps, quotas, verification, security controls or approval rules, and no use of another customer's resources.
- No multiple, fake or linked accounts to obtain more free or promotional credit, to abuse the affiliate program, or to get around limits, a suspension or a ban.
- No resale of the Services as your own hosting without a written agreement with us.
- No activity that degrades the platform for others, such as sustained saturation of shared network or storage, unless you have a plan for it.

## 6. Content you host for others

If your users can upload or publish content, you are responsible for enforcing this policy on them, for responding to abuse reports, and for taking down illegal content when notified.

## 7. Enforcement

We investigate suspected breaches as described in the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). Depending on the severity, we may warn you, remove content, block traffic, suspend a resource or the whole account, or terminate the agreement.

Illegal activity, fraud and serious or repeated breaches lead to immediate suspension without prior notice, termination, and a permanent ban of the person, the company and any related accounts, payment methods and identities, as set out in section 10 of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). We may withhold or offset amounts to cover losses and costs as the law allows, forfeit promotional credit, report the conduct to the competent authorities, and take legal action, including claims for compensation.

In other cases we notify you and give you a reasonable time to fix the problem.

## 8. Reporting abuse

Report abuse to **support@progrid.sa** with "Abuse report" in the subject line. Include the Progrid IP address, domain or URL, the date and time with time zone, a description of the problem, and logs or headers as evidence. We read reports every day and may share the report with the customer concerned, without your contact details unless you agree or the law requires. Copyright notices follow the [Copyright and intellectual property policy](/legal/copyright).

We cooperate with competent authorities as the law requires, as described in our [Law enforcement guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement).
