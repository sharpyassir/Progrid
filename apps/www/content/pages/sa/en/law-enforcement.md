These guidelines explain how Progrid Arabia ("Progrid") responds to requests for customer information from government and law enforcement authorities. They are for information and do not create rights for any person. We may change them as the law and our practice evolve.

## 1. What Progrid holds

Progrid provides cloud infrastructure. Depending on the account, we may hold:

- account and billing information, such as name, email address, phone number, company, tax details, invoices and payment references;
- technical records, such as sign in records, IP addresses, audit logs and records of resources created;
- content that customers store on their servers, volumes, databases and buckets.

Customers control their own content and often encrypt it. We do not generally have access to it and may not be able to provide it. In many cases the customer is the right party to receive a request for content.

## 2. Requests from Saudi authorities

We disclose customer information only in response to a valid legal request from a competent authority of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia that is issued in line with the applicable laws, such as the Anti-Cyber Crime Law and the Law of Criminal Procedure.

A request should:

- be in writing, on official letterhead, sent from an official channel or email address;
- identify the requesting authority, the official responsible and the legal basis for the request;
- identify the account precisely, for example by email address, IP address, domain or resource identifier;
- describe the information requested and the time period, as narrowly as possible.

We review each request for its legal validity and scope. We may refuse or narrow requests that are unclear, too broad or not legally valid, and we may ask the authority to clarify.

## 3. Requests from foreign authorities

Foreign authorities should make their requests through official channels recognized by the laws of the Kingdom, such as mutual legal assistance arrangements or the competent Saudi authorities. We do not respond directly to foreign requests unless the law allows us to.

## 4. Emergencies

If we believe in good faith that there is an imminent risk of death or serious physical harm, we may disclose limited information to a competent authority without a formal request, as the law allows. Emergency requests must explain the nature of the emergency and should be followed by a formal request.

## 5. Notice to customers

We notify the customer of a request concerning their account before disclosing information, unless the law or a court order prohibits it, or notice would endanger a person or hinder an investigation of a serious crime. Where notice was delayed, we notify the customer when the restriction ends.

## 6. Preservation

On a valid request from a competent authority, we may preserve specific records for a limited period, normally 90 days, while the authority obtains the legal order needed for disclosure. Preserved data is disclosed only on valid legal process.

## 7. Cooperation on abuse and fraud

Separately from requests, we may report illegal activity, fraud and abuse on our platform to the competent authorities and cooperate with them, as described in section 10 of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). Child sexual abuse material is always reported.

## 8. Personal data

We handle all requests in line with the Personal Data Protection Law and our [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy), and we disclose only the minimum information required.
