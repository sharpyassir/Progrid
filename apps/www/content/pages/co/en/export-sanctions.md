This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms) of Progrid Technologies LLC ("Progrid"). Progrid is a US company and must comply with US export control and sanctions laws, and with the other regimes that apply to it, its providers and its customers. You must comply with them too.

## 1. Applicable regimes

Including, as they apply:

- US Export Administration Regulations (EAR), administered by the Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security.
- US economic sanctions administered by the Department of the Treasury, Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).
- Sanctions and export controls of the European Union and its member states, including Germany, where our infrastructure is located.
- Sanctions and export controls of the United Kingdom.
- Sanctions adopted by the United Nations Security Council.
- The local laws that apply to you.

## 2. Sanctioned countries and regions

You may not use the Services, open an account, or allow anyone to use your account, from, in, or for the benefit of any country or region subject to comprehensive sanctions. These currently include Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, as updated by the authorities. The list changes over time, and the current official lists apply.

You may not use VPNs, proxies or other means to hide your location in order to get around this rule.

## 3. Restricted parties

You may not use the Services if you, or anyone who owns or controls you, or anyone you act for, is:

- on the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List (SDN) or another OFAC list;
- on the BIS Entity List, Denied Persons List, Unverified List or Military End User List;
- on the EU, UK or UN consolidated sanctions lists; or
- otherwise the target of sanctions, including entities 50 percent or more owned by listed persons.

You may not use the Services to provide services, data or funds to any such person.

## 4. Prohibited end uses

You may not use the Services for:

- the development, production or use of nuclear, chemical or biological weapons, or missiles that can deliver them;
- military end uses or military end users where the law restricts them;
- any other end use that requires an export license we do not have; or
- exporting or transferring software, technology or data in breach of export controls, including through your resources.

## 5. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the classification and export compliance of the data, software and technology you store on or send through the Services, and for screening your own customers and users. You confirm that the information you give us, including your billing country and location, is true.

## 6. Our rights

To comply with these laws, we may:

- screen accounts, payments and IP addresses against sanctions lists and country data;
- ask for information about your identity, ownership, location and intended use;
- block sign ups, access and payments from sanctioned countries and regions;
- refuse, suspend or terminate accounts immediately and without prior notice, without liability, where we reasonably believe this policy has been breached or that continuing would expose us to sanctions risk;
- freeze or block funds or property where the law requires it, and report to the authorities; and
- keep records of accounts, transactions and screening results for as long as the law requires, which may be after your account is closed.

Fees paid before a termination under this policy may not be refunded where the law prohibits it.

## 7. Questions

If you are unsure whether this policy applies to you, write to **support@progrid.co** before you use the Services.
