This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms) between Progrid Technologies LLC ("Progrid", "processor") and the customer that accepted them ("Customer", "controller"). It applies automatically, without signature, whenever Progrid processes Customer Personal Data in providing the Services. Customers whose billing country is Saudi Arabia contract with Progrid Arabia, whose own terms apply.

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of service or another policy, this DPA prevails for the processing of personal data.

## 1. Definitions

Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"). "Data Protection Law" means all laws that apply to the processing, including the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA), and similar laws. "Customer Personal Data" means personal data in Customer Content, as defined in the Terms of service, that Progrid processes for Customer. "Subprocessor" means a third party Progrid engages to process Customer Personal Data. "Security Incident" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and scope

**2.1 Roles.** Customer is the controller of Customer Personal Data, or a processor acting for its own controllers. Progrid is the processor, or a subprocessor where Customer is a processor. Where Customer is a processor, Customer confirms that its instructions are authorized by its controller.

**2.2 Scope.** This DPA covers Customer Personal Data only. Personal data that Progrid processes as a controller, such as account, billing, verification and usage data about Customer and its users, is covered by the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

**2.3 Customer obligations.** Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of the processing, for having a legal basis and giving any notices and obtaining any consents required, for the accuracy of Customer Personal Data, and for configuring the Services, including access control, backups and encryption at rest where needed, to suit its data.

## 3. Instructions

Progrid processes Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, Customer's configuration and use of the Services, and support requests are Customer's complete instructions. Progrid may also process Customer Personal Data where required by law, in which case it informs Customer first unless the law prohibits it. Progrid informs Customer if it believes an instruction breaches Data Protection Law, and may suspend that processing until the instruction is confirmed or changed.

Progrid does not access the contents of Customer's servers, volumes, databases or buckets except as needed to operate and secure the Services, to respond to a support request Customer made, to investigate a suspected breach of the Agreement, or as required by law.

## 4. Confidentiality of personnel

Progrid ensures that personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations or an appropriate legal duty of confidentiality, are vetted before they receive access, receive security and privacy training, and have access only as needed for their role.

## 5. Security

Progrid implements and maintains the technical and organizational measures in Annex 2, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing, as well as the risks to data subjects. Progrid may update the measures, provided that the overall level of security is not reduced. Customer is responsible for the security of what it controls, including its operating systems, applications, credentials, tokens, AI agents and backups.

## 6. Subprocessors

**6.1 Authorization.** Customer gives Progrid general authorization to engage Subprocessors. The current list is on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page and is approved by Customer.

**6.2 Obligations.** Progrid imposes on each Subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations that are no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible to Customer for its Subprocessors' performance.

**6.3 Changes.** Progrid gives at least 30 days' notice by email to the account owner before a new Subprocessor begins processing Customer Personal Data, and updates the Subprocessors page at the same time. Other contacts can ask to receive these notices at support@progrid.co.

**6.4 Objection.** Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds by writing to support@progrid.co within 30 days of the notice. The parties will discuss the concern in good faith. If Progrid cannot offer a reasonable alternative, Customer may terminate the affected Services by notice before the change takes effect, and Progrid refunds any unused prepaid credit that Customer bought for them.

**6.5 Emergency replacement.** Where a Subprocessor must be replaced urgently for security or continuity reasons, Progrid notifies Customer as soon as possible and the objection right above applies.

## 7. Assistance

**7.1 Data subject requests.** Customer controls Customer Personal Data and can access, correct, export and delete it through the Services. If Progrid receives a request from a data subject about Customer Personal Data, it refers the data subject to Customer and does not answer it unless Customer instructs it to or the law requires it. Progrid provides reasonable assistance to Customer in answering requests it cannot answer through the Services.

**7.2 Impact assessments and consultations.** Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, Progrid provides reasonable assistance with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities, mainly by providing this DPA, its documentation and the information in Annex 2.

**7.3 Costs.** Progrid may charge reasonable costs for assistance that goes beyond what the Services and documentation provide, with notice in advance.

## 8. Security Incidents

**8.1 Notice.** Progrid notifies Customer of a Security Incident without undue delay, and where feasible within 72 hours, after becoming aware of it. Notice is sent to the email of the account owner and, where possible, shown in the console.

**8.2 Content.** The notice describes, to the extent known, the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. Where information is not yet available, Progrid provides it in stages.

**8.3 Response.** Progrid takes reasonable steps to contain and investigate the incident and to reduce its effects, and cooperates with Customer's own notifications. Progrid's notice is not an admission of fault. Unsuccessful attempts that do not compromise the security of Customer Personal Data, such as pings, port scans or failed sign in attempts, are not Security Incidents.

## 9. Deletion and return

During the term, Customer can export and delete Customer Personal Data through the Services. When Customer deletes a resource, its data is deleted. When the Agreement ends, Progrid deletes remaining Customer Personal Data at the latest 30 days after the account is closed, except for backups, which expire on their normal schedule, and except where the law requires Progrid to keep it. Data that Progrid must keep remains protected by this DPA. On request, Progrid confirms deletion in writing.

## 10. Audits

**10.1 Information.** On written request, no more than once a year, Progrid provides the information reasonably necessary to show compliance with this DPA, such as security documentation, policies, and third party audit reports or certifications where available, including those of its hosting Subprocessors. This information is Progrid's confidential information.

**10.2 On site audits.** Where the information provided is not enough to show compliance, or where a supervisory authority requires it, Customer may audit Progrid, by itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, no more than once a year, with at least 30 days' written notice, during business hours, without access to other customers' data, and in a way that does not disrupt the Services. The parties agree the scope, timing and duration in advance. Customer bears its own costs and pays Progrid's reasonable costs unless the audit reveals a material breach by Progrid. Audits of Subprocessor facilities are carried out through the Subprocessor's own audit reports.

## 11. International transfers

**11.1 Location.** Customer Personal Data in Customer's resources is hosted in Germany: customer compute on Hetzner Online in Falkenstein, and the control plane on DigitalOcean in Frankfurt. Some Subprocessors process limited data outside the EU, as listed on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

**11.2 EU Standard Contractual Clauses.** To the extent Customer Personal Data subject to the GDPR is transferred to Progrid, or by Progrid to a Subprocessor, in a country without an adequacy decision, the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 ("SCCs") are incorporated into this DPA by reference, as follows: Module 2 (controller to processor) where Customer is a controller, and Module 3 (processor to processor) where Customer is a processor. Clause 7 (docking) applies. Under Clause 9, option 2 (general authorization) applies with the notice period in section 6.3. The optional wording in Clause 11 does not apply. Under Clauses 17 and 18, the SCCs are governed by the law of, and disputes are resolved by the courts of, Ireland. Annex I of the SCCs is completed by Annex 1 of this DPA, and Annex II by Annex 2.

**11.3 UK.** For transfers subject to the UK GDPR, the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner ("UK Addendum") is incorporated by reference. Tables 1 to 3 are completed with the information in this DPA and its Annexes, and either party may end the UK Addendum as allowed by its Section 19.

**11.4 Switzerland.** For transfers subject to Swiss law, the SCCs apply with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner as the competent supervisory authority and with references to the GDPR read as references to Swiss law.

**11.5 Government access.** Progrid reviews requests from public authorities for Customer Personal Data under its [Law enforcement guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement), challenges requests it considers unlawful, discloses only the minimum required, and notifies Customer unless the law prohibits it.

## 12. CCPA service provider terms

To the extent Customer Personal Data is personal information under the CCPA, Progrid acts as a service provider. Progrid will not: sell or share it; retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the business purposes of providing the Services under the Agreement, or outside the direct business relationship with Customer; or combine it with personal information it receives from others, except as the CCPA allows. Progrid complies with the CCPA's obligations for service providers, provides the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires, notifies Customer if it can no longer meet these obligations, and allows Customer to take reasonable steps to stop and remedy unauthorized use. Progrid certifies that it understands these restrictions.

## 13. Liability and term

Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA, including under the SCCs to the extent the law allows, is subject to the limitations and exclusions in the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). This DPA lasts as long as Progrid processes Customer Personal Data. Progrid may update this DPA as described in the Terms of service, but will not reduce the protection of Customer Personal Data except where required by law.

## Annex 2: Technical and organizational measures

**Encryption.** Data in transit between users and the Services is encrypted with TLS. Traffic between the control plane and the hypervisor hosts runs through an encrypted WireGuard tunnel. Some traffic between Progrid components on the same private network is not yet encrypted, and Progrid is adding encryption to it. Passwords are stored as salted hashes (argon2id) and API tokens as hashes. Credentials and other secrets that the Services store for Customer are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, under keys kept separately from the database. Control plane backups are encrypted with age before they leave Progrid's servers. Full disk encryption of the storage of the hypervisor hosts and of the control plane is planned and not yet in place. Customers can encrypt their own volumes and databases at rest.

**Access control.** Access to production systems is limited to named personnel who need it for their role, under least privilege, is reviewed every quarter, and is removed promptly when no longer needed. Administrative access to the Services requires two factor authentication. Engineers reach servers covered by managed services only through time limited, approved access grants, with short lived certificates issued per session through a gateway, never with permanent keys. Access to Progrid's own servers uses personal keys over restricted network paths, and password login to servers is disabled.

**Session recording and audit logs.** Engineer sessions on servers covered by managed services are recorded and the recordings are kept for 12 months. Every action that changes data through the console and the API, and every read by Progrid staff in its administrative tools, is written to an audit log that is append only, protected by a hash chain so that changes can be detected, and kept for 1 year.

**Network security.** Host firewalls deny inbound traffic by default and open only what each service needs. Hypervisor management interfaces and internal services are reachable only over a private, encrypted management network, and Progrid's administrative functions require two factor authentication. Each customer server runs in its own virtual machine, and firewall rules enforced on the hypervisor, outside the customer's control, restrict traffic between different customers' networks. Applications of different customers on the application platform run in separate container networks with restricted privileges and cannot reach Progrid's internal networks.

**Availability and backups.** The control plane databases are backed up every night and the backups are encrypted. Off site backup copies and monthly restore tests are being introduced. Services are health checked; centralized monitoring and alerting are being introduced. Customers can take snapshots of their resources; snapshots are stored on the same infrastructure as the resource and are not an off site backup.

**Physical security.** The Services run in data centers operated by DigitalOcean (Frankfurt, Germany) and Hetzner Online (Germany), which provide physical access control, power and cooling redundancy and environmental protection. Storage devices that leave Progrid's control are erased before they are returned to the provider.

**Personnel.** Progrid requires personnel with access to production systems or Customer Personal Data to be bound by confidentiality obligations, to be screened before receiving that access as permitted by applicable law, and to receive security and privacy training when they join and every year.

**Vulnerability management.** Operating system security updates are installed automatically on Progrid's servers. Code, dependencies and container images are scanned automatically for vulnerabilities and leaked secrets before release. Vulnerabilities are fixed within defined periods according to their severity. Security reports from researchers are accepted at security@progrid.co.

**Incident response.** A documented incident response plan covers detection, containment, investigation, notification under section 8, and follow up.

**Subprocessor management.** Progrid assesses the security of its Subprocessors, binds them by written data protection terms, and reviews them every year.

**Data minimization and deletion.** Progrid does not read or index Customer Content, except where needed to provide support or managed services that Customer requests, or where the law requires it. Deleted resources are removed from active systems, and backups expire on their schedule.
