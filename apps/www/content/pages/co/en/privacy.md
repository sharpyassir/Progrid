> **Draft for legal review.** This policy will be reviewed by counsel for the laws that apply to Progrid Technologies LLC and its customers before launch.

Progrid Technologies LLC ("Progrid", "we") is the controller of the personal data described in this policy for customers whose billing country is not Saudi Arabia. Customers whose billing country is Saudi Arabia are covered by the policy of Progrid Arabia at [progrid.sa/legal/privacy](https://progrid.sa/legal/privacy). Questions and requests: **support@progrid.co**.

## What we collect

**Account data.** Name, email address, password (stored as a hash), phone number if you add one, company name, tax number and address for invoicing, your billing country, and the identity or business documents you provide for verification.

**Billing data.** Invoices, payments and credit history. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we receive a reference, the last four digits and the card brand, never the full number.

**Usage and technical data.** API requests, console actions, IP addresses, browser and device information, and an audit log of who did what on your account, including API tokens and the AI tools you connect. Server metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network) are collected to run the platform and show you graphs. Our website looks up the country of your IP address in a local database to suggest the right storefront; the lookup does not leave our servers.

**Support data.** Tickets, messages and the details you give us to solve a problem.

**Content you store.** Whatever you put on your servers, volumes, databases and buckets is yours. We do not read it, index it or use it for any purpose other than storing and serving it, except as needed to respond to your support request or as required by law.

## Why we process it

- To provide the Services and keep them secure: account management, metering, invoicing, fraud and abuse prevention, incident response.
- To meet legal obligations: tax and accounting records, and lawful requests from competent authorities.
- To communicate with you: service notices, invoices, security alerts, and answers to your requests. Marketing email only with your consent, and you can withdraw it from any message.
- To improve the Services, using aggregated and de-identified data.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effect on you.

## Who we share it with

- **Payment processing:** our payment provider, for card payments.
- **Progrid Arabia,** the Progrid company for customers in Saudi Arabia, when your account moves to it because your billing country changes, or to operate shared parts of the platform under the same protections.
- **Infrastructure and communication providers** that host parts of the platform or deliver email and SMS, under contracts that bind them to protect the data.
- **Authorities**, when a lawful request or court order requires it. We notify you unless the law prevents it.
- **A successor**, if Progrid is acquired or merges, under the same protections.

## Where your data is stored

Our infrastructure providers store and process data for us on their servers, which may be in other countries than yours. Where data moves between countries, we use the safeguards the applicable law requires.

## How long we keep it

Account data for as long as your account exists and for 5 years after closure to meet accounting and tax rules. Audit logs for 1 year. Server metrics for 30 days at full resolution. Support tickets for 3 years. Content you store is deleted when you delete the resource and, at the latest, 30 days after your account is closed, except for backups which expire on their normal schedule.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it where we have no legal duty to keep it, to give you a copy in a usable format, and to stop processing it in certain cases. Send requests to support@progrid.co from the email on your account. We answer within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Access to production systems is limited to named engineers with two step sign in and is logged. We notify you and, where required, the authorities of a personal data breach without undue delay.
