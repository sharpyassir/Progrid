## 2. What we collect

**Account data.** Name, email address, password (stored as a hash), phone number if you add one, company name, tax number and address for invoicing, your billing country, team membership and roles, and sign in method. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider.

**Legal acceptance record.** When you accept the Terms of service, the Acceptable use policy and this policy, or a new version of them, we record the version of the documents, the date and time, your IP address and your browser or client user agent.

**Identity, verification and fraud data.** The identity or business documents you provide for verification, the results of our checks, risk signals about sign ups and payments (such as IP address, country, device and browser details, email domain, and links between accounts), and records of suspensions and bans.

**Billing data.** Invoices, payments, credit and refund history. Card payments are processed by Stripe. Card data goes directly to Stripe and never touches our servers; we receive a reference, the last four digits and the card brand, never the full number.

**Affiliate data.** If you join the affiliate program: your partner code, referrals, commission, payout details (bank account), and the tax forms and identity documents we ask for before paying.

**Usage and technical data.** API requests, console actions, IP addresses, browser and device information, and an audit log of who did what on your account, including API tokens, Progrid Connect agents and the AI tools you connect. Server metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network) are collected to run the platform and show you graphs. Our website looks up the country of your IP address in a local database to suggest the right storefront; the lookup does not leave our servers.

**Communications and support data.** Tickets, emails, and the details you give us to solve a problem. For managed cloud services, the on call contacts and phone numbers you give us for SMS or WhatsApp alerts.

**Integration data.** If you connect GitHub for Git deploy, we receive the account identifier and the access you grant, and the repository data needed to build and deploy.

## 3. Where we get it

From you, when you sign up, use the Services, buy, or contact us. Automatically, from your devices and our systems. From third parties: Google or Microsoft when you choose to sign in with them, GitHub when you connect it, Stripe for payment status and fraud signals, a team owner who invites you, and an affiliate who referred you.

## 4. Why we use it and our legal bases

Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Article 6), we rely on these legal bases:

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Create and run your account, provide the Services, meter usage, invoice and collect payment | Account, billing, usage | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Record your acceptance of our terms and policies | Acceptance record | Legitimate interests in proving the agreement (Art. 6(1)(f)), and legal obligations where they apply (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Keep the Services secure, prevent fraud and abuse, verify identity, enforce our terms and bans | Verification, fraud, usage, acceptance record | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), and legal obligations where they apply |
| Tax, accounting and legal records, responses to lawful requests, sanctions screening | Account, billing, affiliate, verification | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Service notices, security alerts, invoices, support answers, on call alerts | Account, support, phone numbers | Performance of a contract, and legitimate interests |
| Run the affiliate program and pay commission | Affiliate data | Performance of a contract, and legal obligation for tax records |
| Improve the Services with aggregated and de-identified data | Usage | Legitimate interests |
| Marketing email | Email | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw from any message |
| Establish, exercise or defend legal claims | Any relevant data | Legitimate interests |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights. You can ask us for details and you can object (see section 11).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for targeted or cross context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Automated decisions

We use automated fraud screening on sign ups and payments, partly through Stripe. It may block a payment, hold a sign up for review, or limit an account. You can ask for a human to review any such decision, give your point of view and contest it, by writing to support@progrid.co. We do not make other decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

- **Others at your direction,** for example a team owner who can see team activity, or an integration you connect.

You can ask for a copy of the relevant safeguards at support@progrid.co.

## 9. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | As long as your account exists, and 5 years after closure to meet accounting and tax rules |
| Billing and affiliate payout records | 5 years after the end of the year they relate to, or longer where tax law requires |
| Legal acceptance records | As long as your account exists, and 5 years after closure |
| Verification and fraud records | As long as your account exists and 5 years after closure; records of bans for as long as needed to enforce the ban |
| Audit logs | 1 year |
| Server metrics | 30 days at full resolution |
| Support tickets | 3 years |
| Customer content | Deleted when you delete the resource and, at the latest, 30 days after your account is closed, except for backups, which expire on their normal schedule |

We may keep data longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed for a pending legal claim, investigation or preservation request.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Access to production systems is limited to named engineers with two step sign in and short lived certificates, and their sessions and actions are logged. We keep backups and use host firewalls. We notify you and, where required, the authorities of a personal data breach without undue delay. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your credentials and turn on two step sign in.

## 11. Your rights

You can send any request to **support@progrid.co** from the email on your account. We answer within 30 days, or within the shorter or longer period your local law sets. We may need to verify your identity first. We do not charge for requests unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

### EU, EEA and UK

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests (and at any time to direct marketing), and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also complain to the data protection supervisory authority where you live or work, or where an infringement took place. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

### California and other US states

This section applies to residents of California under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA), and to residents of other US states with similar laws, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and Texas.

**Categories collected in the past 12 months:** identifiers (name, email, IP address, account identifiers); customer records (address, phone, tax number, payment references); commercial information (purchases, invoices, credit); internet and network activity (usage, logs, audit records); geolocation at country level from IP address; professional information (company name and role); and sensitive personal information limited to account sign in credentials and, where provided, identity documents. We collected them from the sources in section 3, for the purposes in section 4, and disclosed them for business purposes to the recipients in section 6.

**No sale or sharing.** We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of anyone under 16.

**Sensitive information.** We use sensitive personal information only for the purposes the CCPA allows, such as providing the Services, security and fraud prevention, and not to infer characteristics about you.

**Your rights:** to know what we collect, use and disclose, and to get a copy; to delete; to correct; to opt out of sale, sharing and certain profiling (we do not do these); to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we already limit it); and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

**Authorized agents.** You can use an authorized agent. We may ask for proof of the agent's authority and ask you to verify your identity directly.

**Verification.** We verify requests by matching them to your account, normally by a request from the account email and, where needed, further information. If we deny a request, we explain why. Where your state law gives you a right to appeal, reply to our answer and ask for an appeal; if we deny it, you may contact your state attorney general.

### Canada

Under PIPEDA and provincial laws, you can access and correct your data, withdraw consent subject to legal or contractual limits, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial commissioner.

### Australia

Under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, you can access and correct your data. If you are not satisfied with our answer to a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

### Brazil

Under the LGPD, you have the rights in its Article 18, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. You can complain to the ANPD.

### Other places

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, and we will apply the rights that your local data protection law gives you.
