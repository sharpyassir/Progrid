This policy is part of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms). It applies to everything you run on Progrid and to everyone who uses your account, including team members, your own customers and users, scripts, and AI tools or Progrid Connect agents acting with your tokens. You are responsible for their compliance. Breaking this policy leads to suspension, termination and, in serious cases, a permanent ban and legal action, as described below.

The lists below are examples. They do not limit the general rule: nothing illegal, nothing fraudulent, and nothing that harms others or the platform.

## 1. Illegal content and activity

You may not use the Services for anything that is illegal under the laws of the United States, the laws of the State of [to be confirmed], or any other law that applies to you, your users, the people affected, or us. This includes:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that sexualizes, exploits or endangers minors. We remove it at once, terminate the account, preserve the evidence and report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as US law requires, and to other authorities where appropriate.
- Content that promotes, supports or recruits for terrorism or violent extremism, or that incites violence.
- Trafficking in weapons, explosives, controlled drugs, counterfeit goods, or people.
- Gambling, lottery or betting services without a valid license in every jurisdiction where they are offered.
- Unlicensed financial services, money laundering, and the sale of stolen data, credentials or payment cards.
- Any activity that breaches sanctions or export controls, or that helps someone evade them. See the [Export controls and sanctions policy](/legal/export-sanctions).

## 2. Fraud, scams and deception

- Fraud, scams, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, fake shops, and investment or romance scams.
- Phishing, including pages that imitate a bank, a brand, a government or Progrid to collect credentials or payment details.
- Identity theft, impersonation of real people or organizations, and false or borrowed identities used to open or verify accounts.
- Payment fraud, the use of stolen or unauthorized cards or accounts, and chargeback abuse.
- Creating multiple or fake accounts to abuse free credit, trials, referrals, affiliate commission or promotions.

## 3. Security attacks and network abuse

- Malware, ransomware, spyware, exploit kits, and hosting or distributing them.
- Botnets and command and control (C2) servers.
- Denial of service (DDoS) attacks, and hosting booter or stresser services.
- Unauthorized access to any system, network, account or data, and scanning or probing of systems you are not authorized to test. Authorized security testing of your own resources is allowed.
- Credential stuffing, brute force attacks and password spraying.
- IP, email or DNS spoofing, and traffic designed to hide its origin for abuse.
- Open proxies, open mail relays and open DNS resolvers left exposed to the internet, unless you operate a properly secured and authorized service.

## 4. Spam and unsolicited messages

No bulk unsolicited email, SMS or messaging, no harvesting of addresses, and no hosting of content advertised through spam. Mailing lists must be opt in with a working unsubscribe. Port 25 is closed by default; ask support to open it for legitimate mail servers with reverse DNS and SPF in place.

## 5. Intellectual property and privacy

- Infringing copyright, trademarks, patents or other intellectual property, including hosting pirated software, media or license keys. See the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright).
- Collecting or processing personal data without a legal basis, or in breach of privacy law.
- Doxxing: publishing private information about a person without consent, including addresses, phone numbers, identity documents or financial details.
- Covert surveillance or stalkerware.

## 6. Harassment and hate

- Harassment, threats, intimidation and bullying.
- Content that promotes hatred or violence against people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or similar characteristics.
- Defamation.
- Non consensual intimate imagery.

## 7. AI and automation

- Deceptive uses of AI, including deepfakes that impersonate real people without their consent, synthetic content presented as real to mislead, and AI generated fraud or disinformation campaigns.
- Using Progrid Connect agents or any other AI agent or automation to do anything this policy forbids.
- Giving agents broad tokens or instructions that let them act outside the purpose you set, or ignoring reports that your agent is causing harm.

## 8. Abuse of the platform

- Cryptocurrency mining and other proof of work computation, unless we have approved it in writing.
- Attempts to bypass or tamper with metering, billing, spending caps, quotas, rate limits, verification, approval rules or security controls.
- Using another customer's resources or accounts without permission.
- Reselling the Services as your own hosting without a written agreement with us.
- Activity that degrades the platform for others, such as sustained saturation of shared network or storage, unless you have a dedicated plan for it.
- Probing, scanning or testing the Progrid platform itself, except under our security reporting rules on the [contact page](/contact).

## 9. Content you host for others

If your users can upload or publish content, you are responsible for enforcing this policy on them, for responding to abuse reports quickly, and for removing illegal content when you learn of it.

## 10. Enforcement

We investigate reports and suspected breaches. Depending on the severity, we may:

- **Suspend immediately and without prior notice** the affected resource or the whole account, where there is illegal activity, fraud, CSAM, an immediate risk to others or to the platform, or a legal order. In other cases we normally notify you first and give you a reasonable time to fix the problem.
- **Remove or block** content, IP addresses, domains or network traffic.
- **Terminate** the agreement.
- **Ban permanently** the person, the company, and any related or successor accounts, payment methods and identities, as described in section 9 of the [Terms of service](/legal/terms).
- **Withhold, offset or retain** amounts owed and credit to cover losses, chargebacks, fees and costs, to the extent the law allows, and forfeit all promotional credit.
- **Take legal action**, including claims for damages, injunctive relief, and recovery of losses, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees.
- **Report** the conduct to law enforcement and other competent authorities, cooperate with them, and preserve and disclose relevant data as the law allows. See our [Law enforcement guidelines](/legal/law-enforcement).

Repeated breaches, even minor ones, may lead to termination. We are not liable for actions taken in good faith to enforce this policy.

## 11. Reporting abuse

Report abuse to **support@progrid.co** with "Abuse" in the subject line. Include the Progrid IP address, domain or URL, the date, time and time zone, and logs or headers that show the problem. Reports are read every day. Copyright complaints should follow the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright). If you have found CSAM, do not copy or send it to us; give us the location and report it to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org or to your national hotline.

We may share your report with the customer concerned, without your contact details unless the law requires otherwise or you agree.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy as described in the [Terms of service](/legal/terms).
