Progrid Technologies LLC uses the subprocessors below to provide the Services. Each one is bound by a written contract with data protection obligations no less protective than our [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa). This page applies to customers of Progrid Technologies LLC (progrid.co). Customers whose billing country is Saudi Arabia should see [progrid.sa](https://progrid.sa/legal/privacy).

## Current subprocessors

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location of processing | When it applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| DigitalOcean, LLC | Hosting of the control plane: website, console, API and their databases | Frankfurt, Germany | Always |
| Hetzner Online GmbH | Customer compute, storage and networking | Falkenstein, Germany | Always, for resources you create |
| Resend | Transactional email, such as sign in, verification, invoices and alerts | Ireland (EU region); US company | Always |
| Stripe | Card payments and payment fraud screening | United States and other Stripe locations | When you pay by card on progrid.co |
| Anthropic | AI model provider for Progrid Connect | United States | Only when you run Progrid Connect agents; the prompts and data you send to an agent are processed by the model |
| Twilio | SMS and WhatsApp alerts | United States | Only for managed cloud services with on call alerts, for the phone numbers you give us |
| Google | Sign in with Google | United States and other Google locations | Only when you choose to sign in with Google |
| Microsoft | Sign in with Microsoft | United States and other Microsoft locations | Only when you choose to sign in with Microsoft |
| GitHub | Git deploy integration | United States | Only when you connect a GitHub account or repository |

The country of your IP address is looked up in a local database on our own servers. No data is sent to a third party for this.

Card data goes directly to Stripe and never touches our servers.

## Transfers

Where a subprocessor processes personal data outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the safeguards described in the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy) and the [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa), such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum.

## Changes

We give at least 30 days' notice by email before a new subprocessor begins processing customer personal data, and we update this page at the same time. Where a subprocessor must be replaced urgently for security or continuity reasons, we notify you as soon as possible.

## Subscribe or object

To receive notices of changes, write to **support@progrid.co** with "Subprocessor notices" in the subject line. Account owners receive them automatically.

To object to a new subprocessor, write to support@progrid.co within 30 days of the notice and explain your reasons. The objection process is described in section 6 of the [Data processing addendum](/legal/dpa).
