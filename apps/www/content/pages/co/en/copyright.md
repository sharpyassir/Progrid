Progrid Technologies LLC respects intellectual property rights and expects its customers to do the same. Infringing material is prohibited by the [Terms of service](/legal/terms) and the [Acceptable use policy](/legal/acceptable-use). We respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. 512 ("DMCA"), and to similar notices under other laws that apply.

Progrid is a hosting provider. Most content on Progrid is controlled by our customers, not by us. Where we can, we forward a notice to the customer and ask them to act. Where needed, we disable access to the material ourselves.

## 2. Filing a notice

Under 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3), your notice must include:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act for them.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed, or a representative list if several works are covered.
3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it, such as the exact URL, IP address or resource.
4. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

Incomplete notices may not be processed. We may share your notice, including your contact details, with the customer concerned.

## 3. What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the material, normally by asking the customer to remove it and, where they do not, by disabling it ourselves. We notify the customer and send them a copy of the notice.

## 4. Counter notice

If your material was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter notice to our designated agent. Under 17 U.S.C. 512(g)(3) it must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and where it appeared before.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district of your address (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which Progrid may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the notice or their agent.

We forward a valid counter notice to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action to restrain the activity, we may restore the material within 10 to 14 business days after receiving the counter notice.

## 5. Misrepresentation

Under 17 U.S.C. 512(f), anyone who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Consider fair use and seek legal advice before sending a notice or counter notice.

## 6. Repeat infringers

We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of customers who are repeat infringers. We track valid notices against each account and may also terminate an account after a single notice where the infringement is clear or serious. A terminated repeat infringer may not open a new account.

## 7. Trademark and other complaints

For trademark infringement, counterfeit goods, or other intellectual property complaints outside the DMCA, write to support@progrid.co with "IP complaint" in the subject line. Include your contact details, the right you own and proof of it (such as a registration number and country), the location of the infringing material, and an explanation of the infringement. We review each complaint and may forward it to the customer, ask them to respond, or remove or disable the material.
