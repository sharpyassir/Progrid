These guidelines are for law enforcement and government authorities that request information about customers of Progrid Technologies LLC ("Progrid"). They are not legal advice and do not create any rights. Requests about customers whose billing country is Saudi Arabia should be sent to Progrid Arabia through [progrid.sa](https://progrid.sa/contact).

## 1. Our approach

We respect our customers' privacy and we cooperate with valid legal process. We review every request for legal validity and scope, and we disclose only the data that the law requires. We may object to or challenge requests that are overbroad, unclear or unlawful.

## 2. What data we may have

Depending on the account and the Services used, we may hold:

- **Account and subscriber information:** name, email address, phone number, company name, billing country and address, sign up date, and the IP address and user agent recorded when the terms were accepted.
- **Billing records:** invoices, payment references, and the last four digits and brand of a card. Full card numbers are held by our payment provider, not by us.
- **Logs:** console and API access logs with IP addresses and timestamps, and audit logs, which we keep for 1 year.
- **Content:** data customers store on their servers, volumes, databases and buckets. We do not monitor this content. Customers control it and may delete it at any time.

Infrastructure is located in Germany. We may not hold data that has been deleted or has passed its retention period, as described in the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

## 3. Legal process required

We require valid legal process that applies to us:

- **Subscriber information:** a subpoena or equivalent legal process.
- **Non content records,** such as logs: a court order, for example under 18 U.S.C. 2703(d), or equivalent process.
- **Content:** a search warrant issued on probable cause, or equivalent process in the relevant jurisdiction.

Requests must be in writing, on official letterhead, identify the requesting authority and officer, give a reply address on an official domain, identify the account precisely (account email, resource identifier, IP address with date and time, or domain), and describe the data sought and the time period.

## 4. International requests

Authorities outside the United States should use a Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT), letters rogatory, or another valid mechanism such as an agreement under the CLOUD Act. We may also respond to valid local legal process where the law that applies to us allows it. Because customer data is stored in Germany, German and EU law may also apply.

## 5. Emergency requests

We may disclose information without legal process where we believe in good faith that there is an emergency involving imminent danger of death or serious physical injury to a person, and that disclosure without delay is needed to prevent it. Emergency requests must be sent from an official address, explain the nature of the emergency and why the data is needed, and be signed by a sworn officer. We may ask for legal process afterwards.

## 6. Preservation requests

We preserve the data available at the time of a valid preservation request, such as one under 18 U.S.C. 2703(f), for 90 days while legal process is obtained. A preservation may be extended once for another 90 days on a written request. We do not disclose preserved data without valid legal process.

## 7. Notice to users

We notify customers of requests for their data before disclosure, so they can seek legal remedies, unless the law or a court order prohibits notice, or notice would create a risk of death or serious harm, or would be counterproductive in an emergency. Where notice was delayed, we notify the customer once the restriction ends.

## 8. Child safety

We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as US law requires.

## 9. Costs

We may seek reimbursement of reasonable costs of responding, where the law allows.

## 10. Contact

Send requests to **support@progrid.co** with "Law enforcement request" in the subject line. Service of process by mail: Progrid Technologies LLC, [registered address to be confirmed]. Accepting a request by email does not waive any objections or rights, including objections to jurisdiction or proper service.
